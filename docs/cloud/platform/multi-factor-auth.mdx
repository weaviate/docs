Multi-factor authentication (MFA) increases the security of browser logins. MFA is not enabled by default.

Any member can enable MFA for their own account. Organization _Owners_ can also require MFA for all members. See [Require MFA for all members](#mfa-enforcement).

:::note Who can change these settings
Every member manages MFA for their own account. Only organization _Owners_ can see and change MFA enforcement. _Admins_, _Editors_, and _Viewers_ do not see that setting. See [User roles](/cloud/platform/users-and-organizations#user-roles).
:::

### Change your authenticator app

To set up a new authenticator app, open the `Manage MFA` dialog as described in [Disable MFA](#disable-mfa) and enter your 6-digit code. Then click `Update MFA`.

## Disable MFA

You turn off MFA for your own account in the console.

1. Open the <Link to="/go/console?utm_content=cloud">Weaviate Cloud console</Link>.
1. Click on the user menu in the top-right corner of the console.
1. Select `Account Settings`.
1. In the `Multi-Factor Authentication` section, click `Manage`.
1. Enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator application.
1. Click `Remove`.

After that, you sign in with your password only.

If an organization you belong to requires MFA, you cannot turn it off. See [Require MFA for all members](#mfa-enforcement).

## Require MFA for all members {#mfa-enforcement}

MFA enforcement requires every member of the organization to use MFA when they sign in. It is available to every organization. It is off by default.

### Turn on MFA enforcement {#turn-on-mfa-enforcement}

1. Open the <Link to="/go/console?utm_content=cloud">Weaviate Cloud console</Link>.
1. On the [Overview page](/cloud/console-overview), open **Organization settings**.
1. Click the `Users and Members` tab.
1. Below the member list, find the `MFA enforcement` section.
1. Turn on `Require MFA for all members`.
1. In the "Enforce multi-factor authentication?" dialog, click `Enforce MFA`.

:::note
MFA enforcement includes you. If you have not set up an authenticator app yet, you set one up at your next sign-in.
:::

MFA enforcement is an organization setting, but it applies to people. If a person belongs to any organization that enforces MFA, they must use MFA at every sign-in. This is true whichever organization they open.

SSO sign-ins are the exception. See [MFA enforcement and SSO](#mfa-enforcement-and-sso).

### Turn off MFA enforcement {#turn-off-mfa-enforcement}

1. On the `Users and Members` tab, go to the `MFA enforcement` section.
1. Turn off `Require MFA for all members`.
1. In the "Stop enforcing multi-factor authentication?" dialog, click `Stop enforcing`.

Members no longer have to use MFA. Members who set up an authenticator app keep it. Each member manages MFA for their own account. See [Enable multi-factor authentication](#enable-multi-factor-authentication).

## MFA enforcement and SSO {#mfa-enforcement-and-sso}

SSO takes priority over MFA enforcement. With SSO, your identity provider handles sign-in security, including its own second factor.

- When [SSO setup](/cloud/platform/single-sign-on#set-up-sso) completes, MFA enforcement turns off. The [warning before setup](/cloud/platform/single-sign-on#warnings-before-setup) says so.
- After that, the `Require MFA for all members` switch is grayed out.
- SSO sign-ins never ask for a Weaviate Cloud MFA code.

Some people belong to an SSO organization and also to an organization that enforces MFA. After their first SSO sign-in, every sign-in they make is an SSO sign-in. Weaviate Cloud does not ask them for an MFA code, whichever organization they open. The second factor of their identity provider applies instead.
