## What is on the Overview page

- A summary line for the organization you are currently working in.
- The **Database** card, which lists your clusters with their product and deployment badges, and a `Create cluster` button. See [Create a cluster](/cloud/manage-clusters/create).
- The **Engram** card, for [memory projects](/engram).
- **Organization settings**, shown inline on the Overview page. This is where *Owners* and *Admins* rename, leave, or delete an organization and manage its [members and roles](/cloud/platform/users-and-organizations#organization-settings).

## Move around the console

- The **product switcher** in the top bar moves between `Overview`, `Database`, and `Engram`.
- The **`CLUSTER` selector** in the left sidebar picks the cluster you are working on, and the `+` button next to the `CLUSTER` label creates a new one. Selecting a cluster opens its `Dashboard`, where you find its endpoints, [API keys](/cloud/manage-clusters/authentication), [metrics](/cloud/manage-clusters/status), and `Advanced configuration`.
- The **user menu** is in the top-right corner. It holds your account settings, including [multi-factor authentication](/cloud/platform/multi-factor-auth).
- **What's New** is in the bottom-left corner.

:::note
The console is under active development, so the layout described here is what you see as of September 2026.
:::
